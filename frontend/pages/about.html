WHO WE ARE

A Home for Heritage, Art and Storytelling.

Sathavahana Kalamkari House was created with a simple appreciation for the richness of Indian textile traditions and the stories they continue to carry through generations.

We believe that traditional art does not belong only in the past. When presented with respect and thoughtful design, it can continue to live beautifully in contemporary spaces, wardrobes and everyday life.

Our collections bring together heritage-inspired patterns, artistic expression and handcrafted character to create pieces that feel meaningful, distinctive and timeless.

THE ART THAT INSPIRES US

More Than Fabric. A Language of Art.

Kalamkari carries centuries of artistic tradition through expressive lines, intricate motifs, natural inspiration and visual storytelling.

Storytelling

Every motif and pattern has the ability to communicate culture, imagination and artistic expression.

Nature

Flowers, vines, birds and organic forms continue to inspire the visual language of Kalamkari.

Craft

The beauty of handcrafted art lies in its individuality, texture and the human touch behind every creation.

Continuity

Traditional art remains alive when new generations continue to discover, appreciate and carry it forward.

OUR VISION

Keeping Heritage Relevant and Timeless.

Our vision is to create a space where people can experience the richness of Kalamkari and Indian artistic traditions in a way that feels approachable, meaningful and contemporary.

We are inspired by the idea that heritage and modern living do not need to exist separately. Traditional artistry can become part of the present when it is celebrated with authenticity and thoughtful presentation.

  • Respect the spirit of traditional art
  • Celebrate handcrafted character
  • Create meaningful contemporary experiences
  • Share the beauty of Indian heritage
