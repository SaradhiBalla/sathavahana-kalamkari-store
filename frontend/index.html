THE ART BEHIND THE CRAFT

Where Every Pattern Tells a Story

Kalamkari is more than a textile tradition. It is a language of line, colour, nature and storytelling passed through generations.

At Sathavahana Kalamkari House, our vision is to present this artistic heritage through a contemporary experience while respecting the spirit of handcrafted Indian textile art.

  • Handcrafted character
  • Artistic storytelling
